The pattern is always the same. I spot a tool, a model or a sensor I've never used, tell myself I'll just have a quick look, and a few hours later there's a half-working prototype on my screen and a long list of things it taught me.

I'm into AI and machine learning, but also plain software engineering, and IoT, which happens to be my degree. I like the places where they meet: a sensor feeding a model feeding a decision. I'll try an LLM one week and an Extended Kalman Filter the next.

Breaking things is part of the process, not a failure of it. Most of what I know I picked up while working out why something stopped working. I care about that process at least as much as the finished thing, which is why this site shows the unfinished stuff too.
